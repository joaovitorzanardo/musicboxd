# Album art: attach a confirmed upload to an Album or Song

## Description

Lets Staff attach a confirmed uploads key as an Album's or Song's cover art, validating the key was issued to that Staff user for that purpose.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verify: Staff uploads an image, attaches it to an Album, and the Album page (once one exists) would resolve the image URL; a key not issued to the caller is rejected.
